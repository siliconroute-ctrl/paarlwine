# If You Like Sancerre, Rioja or Chablis — Drink This From Paarl

The fastest way into an unfamiliar wine region is through a wine you already love. Here are ten familiar reference points and their Paarl counterparts.

## Whites

**You drink Sancerre** (Loire Sauvignon Blanc — crisp, grassy, mineral)
→ **Paarl or Voor-Paardeberg Sauvignon Blanc.** Slightly riper and more tropical, with the same green snap and cutting acidity.

**You drink Vouvray sec** (dry Loire Chenin Blanc — quince, honey, high acid)
→ **Old-vine Paarl Chenin Blanc.** The closest match on this list. Riper stone fruit, the same waxy texture and firm backbone.

**You drink Chablis** (unoaked Burgundian Chardonnay — lean, flinty)
→ **Simonsberg-Paarl unwooded Chardonnay.** A shade fuller, still taut and citrus-driven.

**You drink Condrieu** (Rhône Viognier — apricot, perfume, weight)
→ **Paarl Viognier.** Aromatically identical, usually fresher and much cheaper.

**You drink Champagne**
→ **Method Cap Classique.** Same production method, same grapes, roughly a third of the price.

## Reds

**You drink Crozes-Hermitage or northern Rhône Syrah** (pepper, savoury, medium bodied)
→ **Paarl Mountain Shiraz.** Granite soils give the same peppery savour rather than jammy fruit.

**You drink Rioja Reserva** (Tempranillo — leather, vanilla, dried cherry)
→ **Paarl Shiraz–Cabernet blend with time in oak.** Not the same grape, but a very similar tasting sensation.

**You drink Médoc Cabernet** (blackcurrant, cedar, firm tannin)
→ **Simonsberg-Paarl Cabernet Sauvignon.** A touch riper, same graphite and cedar spine.

**You drink Châteauneuf-du-Pape** (Grenache-led, warm, spicy)
→ **Paarl Rhône blend** — Shiraz, Grenache, Mourvèdre, sometimes Cinsault.

**You drink Zinfandel or a ripe Douro red**
→ **Modern Paarl Pinotage.** Mulberry, smoke and plum, with more freshness than its reputation suggests.

## The reverse works too

Heading overseas from South Africa? The same logic runs backwards: a Paarl Chenin drinker should reach for Vouvray or Savennières in France, and a Paarl Shiraz drinker should look at Crozes-Hermitage or Australian cool-climate Shiraz.
