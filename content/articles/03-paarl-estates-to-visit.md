# 12 Paarl Wine Estates Worth Visiting

Paarl has dozens of cellar doors. These twelve cover the range — old family farms, garden estates, organic pioneers and the ones you can walk to from town.

## For a first visit

**Laborie** — A Cape Dutch homestead a short walk from Paarl's Main Street. The easiest stop if you do not have a car, and strong on Cap Classique.

**Fairview** — Wine and cheese on the slopes of Paarl Mountain, plus the famous goat tower. Rhône varieties are the house style. Busy, and deservedly so.

**Nederburg** — One of the country's best-known names, with a manor house, generous gardens and a deep cellar of noble late harvest wines.

## For serious wine

**Vondeling** — Windswept Paardeberg slopes, mineral whites, elegant Rhône reds and a restored chapel with one of the best views in the district. By appointment.

**Avondale** — Certified organic and biodynamic, with ducks patrolling the vineyards instead of pesticides. Their Chenin Blanc and Cabernet Franc are among the most precise wines in Paarl.

**Glen Carlou** — Chardonnay specialist on the Simonsberg side, with a contemporary art gallery attached.

**Perdeberg** — Dryland bush vine Chenin Blanc from some of the oldest vineyards in the area. Unpretentious and very good value.

## For a long lunch

**Babylonstoren** — Eight hectares of formal garden, two restaurants and a modern winery. Book well ahead; this is a half-day, not a stop.

**Rhebokskloof** — A natural amphitheatre on the north face of Paarl Mountain, with picnics, two restaurants and mountain bike trails.

**Backsberg** — Family estate at the foot of the Simonsberg, carbon-neutral farming, and braai facilities so you can cook your own lunch on site.

## For value and character

**Landskroon** — Seven generations of the de Villiers family, honest Paarl reds and a rare Cape port style, with a terrace looking over the valley.

**Anura** — Wine, cheese and beer under one roof, which makes it the peacekeeper when your group cannot agree.

## Practical notes

- Most tasting rooms open around 10:00 and close between 16:00 and 17:00.
- Book ahead in December, January and over Easter.
- Nominate a driver or hire one. Enforcement is real and tasting portions add up.
- Many estates deduct the tasting fee if you buy a bottle — ask.
